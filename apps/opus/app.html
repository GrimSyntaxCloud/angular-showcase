Opus is a renewed edition of Andres Macias’s Reactive Composer App, originally created for WEB 425 coursework in 2022.

The five original composer records remain. Search by name, explore each record, and return to the collection. Reactive Angular forms make the search respond as you type.

From the original spotlight

Antonio Vivaldi was an Italian composer and violinist known for his concertos. The original project featured his biography; learn more from Encyclopaedia Britannica .
